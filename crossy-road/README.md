This is a variation of Crossy Road with an added timer that pushes the player to keep moving forward, and with a LEGO-style design.

Use the arrow keys to move the character forward, backward, left, and right. Your score increases as you move farther forward, and you lose if you get hit by a vehicle or fail to keep moving forward before the timer runs out.

The AI tool that I used is Chatgpt6-astra (IDE version), my prompting strategy is first asking for a big picture, then making changes on the design. Finally, manually detect bugs and ask AI to fix them.
